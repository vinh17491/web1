04 / HOW I THINK

Engineering beyond the happy path.

01

Server authority over UI assumptions

Authorization belongs at the API boundary. Frontend guards improve navigation; they do not replace backend ownership and role checks.

02

State transitions should be explicit

Orders, payments and automation are easier to reason about when valid transitions and invalid states are visible rather than implicit.

03

Tests are evidence

A feature is stronger when its acceptance conditions, negative paths and regression checks can be inspected.

04

Limitations are part of the design

I prefer an honest boundary over a fake production claim. Case studies include current constraints and what I would improve next.
